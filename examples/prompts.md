# Example requests

These inputs are fictional. Replace them with your own evidence before using the output in a presentation.

## Investor pitch with early usage

```text
Use the pitch skill to prepare a 90-second investor pitch.

Product: a tool that turns weekly maintenance logs into a searchable equipment history.
Audience: investors who understand industrial software, but don't know our workflow.
Evidence: two teams tried the prototype; neither is a paying customer. We have no measured time savings yet.
Demo: show one log becoming a searchable entry with its original source attached.
Goal: a follow-up meeting, without naming a funding amount.

Give me a spoken script, what to show, estimated timing including demo pauses,
and the questions I should be ready to answer. Keep missing evidence explicit.
```

## Customer demo

```text
Use the pitch skill to prepare a 3-minute customer demo.

Product: a scheduling tool for a small repair workshop.
Customer job: see which mechanic can accept a new job without moving existing bookings.
Current product: scheduling and capacity view work; automatic reminders are planned.
Evidence: use the sample calendar I attach. Treat it as sample data.
Goal: ask whether they'd like to evaluate this workflow in a separate meeting.

Give me the spoken script, click-by-click demo cues and a short fallback if the live demo fails.
Don't add a free pilot, a discount or a delivery promise.
```

## Keep a narrow request narrow

```text
Use the pitch skill to revise only this opening for a nontechnical jury: [paste opening].
Keep the product facts, my voice and the existing presentation format.
Return the revised opening and a brief explanation of the change.
```
